# PoolHelp Safety & Disclaimer

**Last updated: July 12, 2026**

PoolHelp helps you keep your water clean and balanced. To do that safely, please read this page. It's short, and it matters.

## Educational information, not professional advice

The guidance in PoolHelp is **educational**. It's based on the well-established TroubleFreePool methodology and common pool-care practice. It is **not** a substitute for professional advice, and it is not a guarantee about your specific pool.

Every pool is different. Use PoolHelp's suggestions as a helpful starting point, and pair them with your own judgment, a physical test kit, and the instructions on your products.

## Verify before you dose

AI and app suggestions can be wrong. Before adding any chemical:

- **Confirm your readings with a physical test kit.** Don't dose based on a single app estimate.
- **Read and follow the label** on every chemical product. The label always wins over the app.
- Add chemicals in reasonable amounts, retest, and adjust gradually. Small and steady beats big and fast.

## Handling chemicals safely

Pool chemicals are useful but can be hazardous if mishandled. Please:

- **Never mix chemicals together** (for example, different chlorine products, or chlorine and acid). Add one chemical at a time, and give it time to circulate before adding another.
- **Always add acid to water, never water to acid.** Pour slowly.
- **Store chemicals safely**: in their original containers, tightly closed, in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place, and **out of reach of children and pets**.
- Wear appropriate protection (eye protection and gloves) as directed by the product label, and avoid breathing fumes.
- **Keep people and pets out of the pool during and after treatment** for as long as the product label specifies.

## When to call a professional

PoolHelp is great for water chemistry, but some things need a licensed professional. Call one for:

- **Electrical** work or anything involving pumps, wiring, or bonding/grounding
- **Gas heaters** and gas lines
- **Structural** issues (cracks, leaks in the shell, decking)
- **Plumbing** repairs and persistent leaks
- Any **persistent problem** you can't resolve, or anything that seems unsafe

When in doubt, stop and get a qualified professional. It's the right call.

## In an emergency

For a medical emergency, chemical exposure, or any immediate danger, **contact your local emergency services or poison control center right away.** Don't wait on the app.
